# Honest playtest review — 2026-10-30

The game has functioning systems, but the current presentation and first-minute experience still read as a prototype. The strongest parts are the distinct mode structure, real faction activity, responsive menu navigation and visible competitive AI battles. The biggest gap is making those systems understandable and readable during play.

## What I actually checked

I used the visible standalone game, rather than relying only on PIE tests. I navigated all three mode flows; selected Mi-Go in Domination, Yig in DOTGOO and Cthulhu in Free Roam; observed a live Domination match with changing point control and many bot-versus-bot eliminations; checked the DOTGOO opening, first ability choice, lane/minion presentation and pause scoreboard; chose Conversion/War/Reconstruction and entered Free Roam; inspected the B belief screen and the autonomous power/strength changes. I tried discrete movement, attack, jump, view and menu inputs through the computer-use interface. Native tests separately verify sustained movement, physical combat hits, camera framing, tower evasion, tactical decisions and match completion rules.

This is a bounded review, not an assertion that everything is certified. The UI driver exposes short key presses rather than held-key play, so I am not presenting it as a full human evaluation of sprint/dash timing or mouse feel. I did not finish three human-controlled matches, audit all sixteen kits, evaluate audio by listening, test a gamepad, run a two-machine session, or certify low-spec performance. No fabricated review score or performance claim is assigned.

## Ten prioritized tasks I can implement

1. **Fix the standalone first spawn and handoff.** A red no-safe-ground message appeared on the initial Domination entry before the director relocated the player. Build the arena before the initial pawn is grounded, validate the final spawn/camera clearance and test the standalone menu-to-match path, not only PIE. Add a recoverable fallback and input-focus check. Done means a fresh launch enters every mode with a valid walking capsule and no transient error banner.
2. **Protect the combat view from nearby bodies.** Returning/nearby allies filled the third-person view, and a first-person view became almost entirely obscured. Add camera-volume occlusion handling for friendly render meshes, stable shoulder framing and crowded-spawn spacing, while preserving physical collision and enemy visibility. Done means nearby allies cannot black out the aiming view.
3. **Finish rig-specific animation mechanics and blending.** The first code-authored motions expose exaggerated poses, abrupt transitions and Yig's independently animated weapon rig. Analyze each hierarchy and joint axis, use bounded distributed tail motion, limb phase/contact and correctly driven weapons; blend transitions. Done means walking/running, takeoff/landing and attacks remain coherent from front, side and rear. This is the next explicitly requested animation pass.
4. **Make damage and danger readable.** Add clear tower range/aggro and release cues, distinguish friendly/enemy effects and make impact/recovery/evade feedback consistent with the actual hitboxes. Preserve skill shots and authored kits. Done means a player can identify who is targeting them and why an attack hit or missed without reading a log.
5. **Improve arena routes and encounter composition.** Grey repeated masonry, broad exposed ground and monumental primitives dominate the views. Create recognizable lane/point landmarks, cover rhythms and deliberate sightlines using original procedural structures and the approved art. Validate every large-body route. Done means players can recognize a route and find a useful combat position at a glance.
6. **Measure and improve squad behavior beyond individual decisions.** Domination produces real battles, but allies can bunch near the player and the DOTGOO opening felt passive in the observed window. Add shared focus/peel/engage decisions, spacing and lane-progress/stuck telemetry; test actual hero-versus-hero engagements across a sustained match. Done means bots support each other without forming a pile or failing to reach useful positions.
7. **Teach the first five minutes through actions.** Mode rules are mostly text banners; Free Roam starts with a broad world and dense belief documentation. Add an optional short objective chain: recruit a follower, witness one belief reward, issue one order, then choose a nearby conflict. For DOTGOO, explain the initial skill choice, lane escort and protected tower order in context. Keep beliefs exclusive to Free Roam.
8. **Turn belief/cult data into player-facing feedback.** The B screen exposes internal event names and implementation wording. Replace the main view with readable benefits/tradeoffs, visible action-to-power changes and loyalty/disobedience explanations; retain full rules in an advanced view. Show where a selected cultist group is working and why an order was refused. Done means the player can understand a power change and correct a failing order without interpreting internal identifiers.
9. **Make character selection communicate release quality and playstyle.** The grid includes model-pending entries, static-source labels, very small/dark portraits and long ability text. Add role/range/control summaries, larger consistent previews and an explicit asset-readiness policy for a published build; preserve all future files. Done means selection accurately previews the character the player will control, with no surprise placeholder presentation.
10. **Add a proper settings and comfort layer.** The visible main/pause flows lack a clear settings entry. Add sensitivity/invert, FOV and camera distance, key remapping, audio controls, readable HUD scale, color-independent team cues and graphics presets with an FPS/frame-time option. Keep settings persistent and test window/resolution/input capture changes. Done means players can make the game comfortable and playable on their own hardware.

These tasks target the three priorities: playable first, then engaging combat and readable decisions, with consistent art direction and motion throughout. Completing a checklist alone does not prove fun; repeat playtests with the owner are needed after each substantial change.

## Correct the range/layout mismatch and test independent pressure — 2026-10-31

The earlier high-overlook clearance fixture used a synthetic 120m projectile. Original ranged kits usually reach 16–25m, so it did not establish useful ground pressure from a 60m-high deck. Four 9m side perches now provide positions within real weapon range; the original high routes serve crossings and drops. The contextual hint no longer advertises ranged pressure from the high deck. No authored kit range was increased.

The new bot role prefers a reachable home-side perch while a teammate captures, with at most one operator per team. An opposite-side high flank requires an otherwise unavailable firing angle, two capturers and a score lead; it is not a longer detour to the same home-side position. Loss of targets, lost capture support, retreat and rally orders end the role. Eligible acid-spit users can actually fire that weapon even when the older alternating bot-slot gate would have skipped it.

The focused native observation requires original-kit projectile contact, not a synthetic long-range bolt or incidental melee damage. It separately covers mirrored ground approaches, low-health/target-loss departure and an autonomous climb/crossing/drop. A full-height Cthulhu also walks all four lower ramps.
