## Defense of the Great Old Ones · Cthulhu

- **18:14 remaining.** Tentacle Slam drew a readable ground warning. A close fight ended with Dagon in the kill feed and Cthulhu at 73 HP. The grounded warning gave the fight a clear beat before impact.
- **18:08 remaining.** Four Deep Ones rose in pink bursts and took up the push. Following a close-range finish with a summon felt like taking control of the lane.

- **17:46 remaining.** Cthulhu reached level 12 with one kill and both teams still had all six towers. An allied Dagon was level 1. That split was too large this early to tell whether the fight was turning on decisions or accumulated stats.

## Domination · Mi-Go

- The perch gave me an angle on the approaches and the seal. Its tooltip made it clear that I had to give up elevation to capture.
- **08:50 remaining.** Yuggoth Harvest bloomed on the contested seal. Casting into the fight from above felt distinct from Cthulhu's approach. In third person, Mi-Go's own wings sometimes crossed the aim area. Switching to first person clarified the targets, at the cost of seeing Mi-Go's casting motion.
- A team-control toast lingered while the point showed **CONTESTED — SCORING PAUSED**. Large heroes also sometimes hid targets from one another on the point.

## Changes from this session

- DOTGOO pays less XP for hero kills, assists, minions, and wards. Nearby minion XP reaches farther so lane partners can earn it without standing on the kill.
- The Domination ownership toast clears when control no longer earns points. Ground-targeted damage casts distinguish out-of-range terrain from a crosshair with no surface.
- Mi-Go's third-person camera sits higher and farther off the shoulder to give the wings more room.

These were two match openings against bots. The clock times above are in-game bookmarks. I used the development console to stage positioning because the desktop automation supplied key taps. Combat used the game's inputs. This pass did not measure sustained movement, full-match progression, or balance against human opponents.
