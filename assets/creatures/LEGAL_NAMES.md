# Legal name allowlist — creatures & entities

Owner-provided guideline (Oct 7, 2026, from cthulhureborn.wordpress.com
"Cosmic Horrors" list). RULE: creature/entity names used in-game must come
from this allowlist or be plainly generic English words (ghoul, hound,
tentacle, corpse...). Never invent Mythos-sounding names — they may collide
with still-copyrighted later authors.

## Why this list is safe
H. P. Lovecraft died in 1937. His original creations are generally accepted
as public domain (no copyright renewals on record; scholars incl. S. T. Joshi
conclude PD). All names below originate with Lovecraft himself.

## Allowlist (Lovecraft-original)
Azathoth, Beings From Ib, Bokrug, Cthulhu, Colour Out Of Space,
Dagon and Hydra, Deep Ones, Dholes, Elder Things, Flying Polyps,
Ghasts, Ghouls, Gloon, Gnorri, Great Race of Yith, Gugs, Hastur,
High Priest Not To Be Described, Hunting Horrors, Hypnos, Mi-Go,
Miri Nigri, Moonbeasts, Nath-Horthath, Night-gaunts, Nodens,
Nyarlathotep, Rat-Things, Re-Animated Corpse, Re-Animated Horror,
Shantaks, Shoggoths, Shub-Niggurath, Star-Spawn of Cthulhu, Tamash,
Winged Servants, Yog-Sothoth, Zo-Kalar, Zoogs and the Ancient Zoog.

## Explicitly FORBIDDEN (still under copyright / trademark risk)
- Hounds of Tindalos — created by Frank Belknap Long (1931), NOT public domain.
- Anything coined by August Derleth or other post-Lovecraft authors.
- The phrase "Call of Cthulhu" as a title/brand — trademarked by Chaosium
  for game products. (Our game is CULT-ULHU; keep it that way.)
- Copying any MODERN depiction (film/game/comic art) of these creatures.
  Our models must be CC0-licensed and our depictions original/generic.

## Notes
- "Cthulhu" as a character name drawn from the 1928 public-domain story is
  fine; the risk zone is trademarks on titles/branding, not the name itself.
- This file is a guideline, not legal advice. When in doubt, use a generic
  descriptive name instead of a Mythos one.
