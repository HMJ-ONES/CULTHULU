## Implementation boundaries
- **Never replace user-approved final choices without confirming the model is missing/unobtainable or implementing only a labeled provisional fallback.** Preserve exact character IDs and existing gameplay.
- **Priority:** Mother Hydra presently has no final 3D selection; Abyssal Siren may be adapted as a *provisional* playable candidate only after an authorized download and independent license/source verification. High polygon count (~761K) requires remesh/retopology and baking. Do not assume rig/animations exist.
- **Bokrug:** user currently selected Four-Legged Frost Predator; downloader listed by creator profile but commercial license not verified. Mountain Dragon from the Fab free pack is a good animated fallback; do not forcibly replace chosen Frost Predator.
- **The Human Deer:** uploaded as original, inspired by Moder from film The Ritual; evaluate design independence before commercial use, and consider environmental monster/NPC or Shub-Niggurath-adjacent thematic use, not an automatic main-roster replacement.
- **Future characters Bast, Hypnos, Elder Mind, Howling Eye are explicitly coming later**; do not add them to current playable selection without request.
- **Ghatanothoa** currently has *provisional Biblical Beast Behemoth*; avoid rejected Overlord-Dark-Young copyrighted design.
- Fab 'NoAI' assets can be used in conventional game design under their license, but never as inputs to generative AI datasets/tools; don't confuse using a programming assistant to write code with submitting a protected asset to model generation.
- Do not promise animations from retopology/voxel remesh alone. Preserve rig topology and skin weights when possible; for static models, rig and animate by licensed clips/manual Blender/Unreal workflows, confirming actual results.
- Record in a manifest each URL, legal source, exact license, attribution, files, skeleton, clips, import paths and screenshot/test result, marking BLOCKED where evidence is missing.
